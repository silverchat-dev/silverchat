# silverchat ($SC)

> "The more zipcoins you burn, the more people it polls." (*Snowmoon*, ch. 27)

The polling network from Vitalik Buterin's novel [Snowmoon](https://vitalik.eth.limo/snowmoon/html/), built on Ethereum. Not affiliated with the author.

Pay $ZC to ask the network a question. Holders answer with a signature and earn a share of what you paid. The result goes on-chain, and nobody can change it after that.

Trust: the poster key decides who is paid from the answerers' 35% of a poll, and nothing else. If it never fixes a result, the asker can take the whole payment back 7 days after the poll closes. The contracts are unaudited.
